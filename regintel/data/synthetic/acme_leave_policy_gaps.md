## 1. Eligibility
Employees are eligible for family and medical leave after 12 months of employment and at least 2,000 hours of service during the 12 months before leave begins, at a worksite with 50 or more employees within 75 miles.

## 2. Amount of Leave
Eligible employees may take up to 8 workweeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a 12-month period, measured backward from the date leave is used.

## 3. Qualifying Reasons
Leave may be taken for the birth and care of a newborn child, placement of a child for adoption or foster care, to care for a spouse, child or parent with a serious health condition, for the employee's own serious health condition, and for qualifying exigencies and military caregiver leave.

## 4. Employee Notice
When the need for leave is foreseeable, employees must provide at least 60 days advance notice before leave begins.

## 5. Health Benefits
Group health plan coverage is maintained during leave on the same terms as if the employee had continued to work.

## 6. Job Restoration
On return from leave, employees are restored to the same or an equivalent position with equivalent pay, benefits and terms of employment.

## 7. Non-Retaliation
Acme Lending does not interfere with, restrain or deny the exercise of leave rights, and does not discharge or discriminate against employees for using leave.

## 8. Records
Leave records are kept for at least 3 years.
