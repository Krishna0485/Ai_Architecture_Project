## 1. Non-Discrimination
Globex Credit does not discriminate against any applicant on a prohibited basis, including race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status or age, in any aspect of a credit transaction.

## 2. Application Information
Loan officers ask each applicant for their religion and national origin during the interview to complete the customer profile.

## 3. Notification of Action Taken
Applicants are notified of approval, counteroffer or adverse action within 45 days after a completed application is received. Adverse action notices include a statement of specific reasons.

## 4. Record Retention
Application records, including any adverse action notices, are retained for 12 months after the applicant is notified of the action taken.

## 5. Appraisals
Applicants receive a copy of all appraisals and written valuations promptly upon completion, and in any event at least three business days before consummation.
