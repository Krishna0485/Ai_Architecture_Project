## 1. Program Governance
Acme Lending designates the Chief Information Security Officer as the Qualified Individual responsible for overseeing, implementing and enforcing the information security program.
The Qualified Individual reports in writing to the Board of Directors at least annually on the overall status of the information security program and material matters.

## 2. Risk Assessment
The Information Security Office performs a written risk assessment every year that identifies reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks to customer information and evaluates the sufficiency of safeguards.

## 3. Access Control
Access to customer information is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly.
Users authenticate to internal systems with a username and a strong password that is rotated every 90 days.

## 4. Encryption
All customer information is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest using AES-256.

## 5. Data Retention and Disposal
Customer records are retained for 7 years after account closure and are then reviewed for disposal.

## 6. Monitoring and Testing
Activity of authorized users is logged and monitored to detect unauthorized access to customer information.
An independent firm performs penetration testing annually, and vulnerability assessments are performed every six months.

## 7. Training
All staff complete security awareness training at onboarding and annually.

## 8. Service Providers
Service providers with access to customer information are selected for their ability to maintain safeguards, are contractually required to implement them, and are periodically assessed.

## 9. Change Management
Changes to information systems follow a documented change management procedure with approval and testing.

## 10. Incident Response
Acme Lending maintains a written incident response plan covering goals, roles, communications, remediation and post-incident review.
Security incidents affecting customers are reported to the Federal Trade Commission within 90 days of discovery when legal review concludes notice is appropriate.
